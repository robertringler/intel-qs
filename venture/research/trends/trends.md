# Structural vs cyclical trends (2026+)

| Trend | Classification | Evidence | Implication for PayeeProof |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI-scaled social engineering (BEC, voice/video deepfakes) | **Structural** | IC3 2025 >$30M BEC with AI nexus (S002); Greenhouse: 31% of hiring managers saw suspected deepfakes (S051) | Callbacks alone degrade; need known-contact verification, account validation and dual control |
| Shift from checks to ACH/Same Day ACH | **Structural** | B2B ACH +10% in 2025; Same Day ACH +16.7% volume (S046) | More fast, final credits; more push-payment fraud surface |
| Regulatory push of fraud duties to originators | **Structural** (rule in force) | Nacha Phases 1–2 (S004, S005) | Durable compliance trigger |
| Insurance hardening on social-engineering cover | Structural/cyclical mix | Sublimits and conditions (S048, S049) | Evidence demand; broker channel |
| Accounting labour shortage | **Structural** | CPA pipeline decline (S053) | Automation of controls is valued; fewer staff to do callbacks |
| AP-suite consolidation and bundling | Structural | BILL/Ramp features (S062, S064) | Competitive compression risk |
| Tariff volatility | Cyclical/political | SCOTUS IEEPA ruling (S027) | Relevant to eliminated trade candidates, not to PayeeProof |
| Agentic AI spend | Hype-heavy, early | Secondary Gartner figures (S039) | Future: agent-initiated payments will need payee assurance; kept as an option, not a thesis pillar |
