# Customer: ICP, jobs-to-be-done, validation plan

**No interviews have been conducted.** Everything here comes from published surveys and bank or insurer guidance,
and is labelled accordingly.

## Ideal customer profile (v1)
- US company with 20–1,000 employees (S057) that pays vendors by ACH or wire.
- Payments are released from a **bank portal** using files generated by an ERP or accounting system
  (NetSuite, Business Central, Sage Intacct, Acumatica, QuickBooks Desktop/Enterprise), or through
  more than one bank [I] (S083).
- Finance team of 1–8 people. Controller or CFO is the buyer, and AP staff are the users.
- Trigger events [I]: the bank sends a Nacha 2026 notice or updates the origination agreement (S076, S077); a
  near-miss or actual BEC loss (S007); the crime or cyber policy renewal questionnaire asks about callbacks (S049);
  an auditor finding.

## Jobs to be done
1. "When a vendor's bank details change, make sure we never pay a fraudster, without slowing AP down."
2. "Before I release today's payment file, tell me which payments go to new, changed or unverified accounts."
3. "When my bank, auditor or insurer asks, prove we have a risk-based fraud-monitoring process and that we followed it."
4. "When staff turn over, keep the control working without tribal knowledge."

## Personas
| Persona | Role in purchase | Pain evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Controller/CFO | Economic buyer | Accountable for losses; only 22% of fraud victims recovered ≥75% of funds (S007) |
| AP specialist | Daily user | Handles vendor change emails; vendor impersonation is the #1 BEC type (S007) |
| Bank treasury officer | Influencer/channel | Must evidence ODFI oversight (S005, S076) |
| Insurance broker | Influencer/channel | Coverage conditioned on verification (S048, S049) |
| Outside auditor / fractional CFO | Influencer | Control testing |

## 90-day validation plan (pre-registered kill metric in falsification.md)
1. Recruit 40 qualified controllers through bank treasury officers, crime-insurance brokers and outsourced-accounting firms.
2. Offer a free NACHA-file screen and vendor-master health check, then ask for a paid pilot at ≥$250/month.
3. Record the conversion rate, objections and willingness-to-pay distribution in the product's own analytics.
4. Kill or pivot if fewer than 5 of 40 convert.
