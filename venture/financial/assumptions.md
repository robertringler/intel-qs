# Financial assumptions

Every driver is a **[M] model assumption** and cites the benchmark it is tied to. Generated outputs are in `scenarios.md`.

| Driver | Pessimistic | Base | Aggressive | Benchmark / basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free signups, month 1 | 25 | 40 | 50 | [M] Product-led entry via free file screen; channel partners drive launch traffic |
| Signup growth MoM (decaying 3.5%/mo) | 8% | 12% | 14% | [M] Content/SEO/partner compounding; decays toward saturation |
| Free→paid conversion | 5% | 8% | 9% | [E] PLG B2B free-to-paid commonly low single digits to ~10% [I]; to be measured in the 90-day test |
| Partner-sourced paid logos/month (from M7) | 1.5 | 4 | 6 | [M] Bank treasury officers, crime-insurance brokers, outsourced accounting firms |
| ARPA per month | $300 | $500 | $650 | [M] Tier mix (strategy/pricing.md); EV model ARPA range $2.4–15k/yr |
| Net expansion per year | 0% | 5% | 8% | [E] SMB NDR median ~97%, mid-market ~108% (S040) |
| Monthly logo churn | 2.0% | 1.2% | 0.9% | [E] SMB 3–7%/mo on monthly plans; annual contracts cut churn 60–75% (S041) |
| Gross margin | 80% | 84% | 86% | [M] SaaS hosting + validation API pass-through + support |
| Blended CAC per paid logo | $8,000 | $5,000 | $4,500 | [E] Mid-market CAC $4.2–6.8k (S040) |
| Base opex/month Y1 (R&D, G&A, support) | $95k | $95k | $110k | [M] 5–6 FTE seed team at loaded ~$180k/yr plus tools, insurance, SOC 2 |
| Opex growth per year | 35% | 35% | 35% | [M] Hiring to support growth |
| Starting cash | $3.0M | $3.0M | $3.0M | [M] Seed round |

Not modelled (would change results): a guarantee or insurance product, bank-channel licences (#2), the TPSP API (#100),
international expansion, and price increases after the evidence network matures.
