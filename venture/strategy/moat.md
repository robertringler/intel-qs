# Moat

AI-commoditization test: *if every competitor got the same frontier model tomorrow, what remains?* Everything that
matters remains, because the value is not generative.

| Moat | Mechanism | Strength today | Strength at 1,000+ customers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workflow ownership | Vendor master, change control and release screening sit in the daily AP path | Medium | High |
| Evidence history | Hash-chained audit trail and annual reviews that auditors and banks rely on | Medium (switching cost) | High |
| Verified-payee network (opt-in) | Cross-customer account reputation (fraud-flagged or verified counts) via privacy-preserving fingerprints | None (cold start) | Medium-high |
| Distribution | Bank and broker partner channel with co-branded kits | Low | Medium |
| Trust/security | SOC 2, encryption, RLS tenant isolation | Built in, not yet audited | Required |

Feature replication **is** easy, as the falsification doc accepts. The defensible core is the network and evidence
accumulation, which only compounds with customer count. That is why penetration is the second most sensitive variable.
