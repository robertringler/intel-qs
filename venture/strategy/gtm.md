# Go-to-market

## Cheapest credible path to first revenue
1. **Free NACHA-file screen** (no card, no integration). Upload today's bank file and see which payments go to new, changed,
   unverified or invalid accounts. This shows value in minutes and converts to a paid vendor-change control.
2. **Channel partners that already hold the trigger event**:
   - *Community-bank treasury officers*: their origination agreements now demand originator processes (S076). Give them
     a co-branded "originator readiness" kit that points to PayeeProof. No bank integration is needed.
   - *Crime and cyber insurance brokers*: coverage is conditioned on documented callbacks (S049). PayeeProof's evidence
     pack is the documentation.
   - *Outsourced accounting and fractional CFO firms*: one firm controls many ICP companies. A partner dashboard is on the roadmap.
3. **Content and SEO on the rule itself**: "Nacha 2026 fraud monitoring for small originators", "vendor bank change
   verification policy template". Search demand is created by the rule (S004) and by bank notices.

## First 10 customers
Design partners recruited through 3–5 community-bank treasury officers and 2 crime-insurance brokers. Founder-led demo,
30-day pilot at $250+/month, weekly feedback. The success metric is the pre-registered 90-day kill test
(research/falsification/falsification.md).

## First 100 customers
- Channel: 10–20 active bank and broker partners, each referring 1–2 customers per quarter.
- Product-led: free screen → Starter self-serve checkout through Stripe.
- Outbound: controllers at firms showing ERP job postings that mention "positive pay" or "ACH" [I].
- CAC by channel: **no channel CAC data exists yet, so channel CAC cannot be estimated from evidence.** The plan uses the
  blended mid-market benchmark ($4.2–6.8k, S040) and replaces it with measured CAC by month 6.

## Sales process
Self-serve up to Starter. A sales-assisted 30-minute demo plus a security packet for Growth and Scale. Security review
materials: architecture/security.md, SOC 2 Type I target in month 9.

## Product-led growth potential
Moderate. Vendor attestation links expose PayeeProof to vendors, who are themselves ICP companies. A "verified by
PayeeProof" vendor page is a viral loop that is scoped but not built.
