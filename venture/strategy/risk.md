# Risk register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Residual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Buyers view the product as paperwork and anchor price low | Medium | High (ARPA is the top sensitivity) | Loss-prevention framing, insurance angle, free screen shows real findings | Medium |
| AP suites or banks bundle "good-enough" verification | Medium-high | High | Rail-agnostic ICP (bank-portal releasers), evidence depth | Medium |
| Eftsure/Trustpair move down-market with self-serve | Medium | High | Speed, price, lower-mid-market UX | Medium |
| Security breach of vendor bank data | Low | Severe | Field-level encryption, RLS, MFA, audit, least privilege, pen test | Low-medium |
| Customer treats "verified" as a guarantee (liability) | Medium | Medium | Contract language, UI wording, E&O insurance | Low |
| Nacha rule softened | Low | Medium | Fraud losses persist regardless of the rule | Low |
| Free US Confirmation-of-Payee network emerges | Low (≤2028) | High | Integrate it as a verification method; evidence layer stays relevant | Medium |
| Funding gap before break-even (base needs a Series A) | Medium | High | Kill gate, capital-efficient variant (peak need $4.0M) | Medium |
| Deepfake voice defeats callbacks | Rising | Medium | Callback only to previously known number, read-back of digits, dual control, account validation | Medium |
