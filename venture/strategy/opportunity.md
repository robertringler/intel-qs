# Opportunity

**PayeeProof** is payee assurance and ACH fraud-monitoring evidence for the lower mid-market.

- **Problem.** Vendor-impersonation BEC redirects outbound payments. Vendor impersonation was reported by 60% of BEC-hit
  organisations (S007). Reported BEC losses were $3.046B in 2025, a floor (S001). Since 22 June 2026 every non-consumer
  ACH originator must operate documented, risk-based fraud monitoring (S004).
- **Customer.** US companies with 20–1,000 employees that release vendor payments from ERP-generated NACHA files or bank
  portals. The controller or CFO buys; AP staff use it.
- **Unsolved gap.** Enterprise tools (Trustpair, Eftsure) are sales-led and quote-priced (S015, S065). AP suites protect
  only their own rails (S062, S064). Banks sell raw validation APIs without workflow or evidence (S063). No product gives
  a 5-person finance team a vendor-change control, a pre-release file screen and an auditor-ready evidence trail for a
  few hundred dollars a month.
- **Why now.** Nacha Phase 2 took effect in June 2026. Insurers condition cover on documented callbacks (S049).
  AI-scaled impersonation is rising (S002). Validation data is API-accessible (S044, S063).
- **Selection.** Ranked first of eight modelled candidates by probability-weighted NPV (mean $8.4M, P(NPV>0) 79%;
  research/statistics/ev_results.md) and survived single-factor stress tests (research/falsification/).
