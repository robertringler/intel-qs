# Business model

- **Revenue**: annual or monthly subscription tiered by outbound payment-volume band and vendor count (see pricing.md).
  Metered pass-through of third-party account validations is added once provider contracts exist.
- **COGS**: hosting (~$1–3k/month at seed scale [M]), validation API pass-through, email delivery, and support (~0.5 FTE per 150
  customers [M]). Target gross margin is 84% [M] (financial/assumptions.md).
- **Unit economics (base)**: ARPA $500/month, CAC $5,000, churn 1.2%/month → LTV (gross margin) ≈ $35k, LTV/CAC ≈ 7,
  CAC payback ≈ 12 months (financial/scenarios.md).
- **Expansion**: tier upgrades as vendor counts grow, multi-entity add-on, API tier for ERP/TPSP integrations (#100),
  and white-label bank edition (#2) as optionality.
- **Retention**: the vendor master of verified payees, accumulated verification evidence and the annual-review record
  are costly to rebuild, and auditors expect continuity. Annual billing is the default.
- **Capital**: $3.0M seed, with a milestone-gated Series A. Kill gate at month 9–12 (financial/model.md).
